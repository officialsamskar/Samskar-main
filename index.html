How much does it cost?

There's no fixed price — it depends entirely on which services you choose, guest count, and how elaborate you want each part to be. Message us on WhatsApp with your occasion and requirements, and we'll work out a quote together.

How far in advance should we book?

The earlier the better, especially during festival and wedding season — but reach out even if your date is close and we'll do what we can.

Do you serve areas outside Bangalore, Mysore, and Shimoga?

These are where we're based, but ask us on WhatsApp — we may still be able to help depending on your location.

Can we book just one service, like catering or decoration alone?

Yes — every service can be booked on its own, or combined into a full package built around your event.
